<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Café B is a regular, community-focused social event held at Biddenham Village Hall. It serves as a popular monthly gathering spot for local residents to meet up, chat and enjoy home-made treats.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Schedule and timing</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The event follows a recurring schedule throughout the year, meeting on the third Tuesday of every month, operating between 10:15 am and 11:45 am.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Refreshments and features</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The café is run on a pop-up basis inside the main hall, offering a simple but welcoming selection of refreshments.
</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list">
    
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Drink selection</strong>: Attendees can choose from a variety of hot and cold beverages, including multiple teas, coffees, hot chocolate and fruit juices.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Food</strong>: The event is well-known for its selection of fresh, home-baked cakes provided by volunteers from the village.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Book swap</strong>: Alongside the food and drink, a dedicated book swap table is set up during the café hours, allowing residents to bring along books they have finished and browse for new reading material.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->
